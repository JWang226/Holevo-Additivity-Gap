Why a scope entry is needed

The manuscript discusses background statements at levels of generality beyond the specialized models required by the channel construction. A theorem for a finite matrix/free-factor representation does not automatically extend to every abstract traced algebra.

Likewise, the proved operational coding equality and weak converse do not assert a strong converse at every rate above capacity.

How to read the completed endpoints

The main finite-channel theorem, coding theorem, and their stated consequences are proved at their attached formal scopes. The broader background entry has no attached declaration proving every discussed generalization.

The exact theorem type, its definitions, and the formalization status notes determine the boundary. The informal construction explains the specialized route without treating those broader assertions as supplied premises.
