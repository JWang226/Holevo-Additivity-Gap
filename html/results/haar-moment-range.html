proved

The actual one-pair trace bound is proved when the true matrix dimension D=N+1 satisfies D >= 2^32 p^80. It supplies the premise of the iterated estimate for finite matrix coefficients and remaining free-group factors. The abstract arbitrary traced C*-algebra formulation is outside the formalized scope.
