One-pair Haar estimate at the original moment range

proved

Weighted chronological counting proves the sharper one-pair Haar estimate at the original dimension threshold.

Mathematical content

For moment order p\ge2 and actual local dimension D\ge2^{32}p^{80}, the strengthened one-pair estimate bounds the real normalized Haar trace moment by the corresponding real free trace moment plus

D^{-1/2}\,\|f_{\mathrm{regular}}\|_{\mathrm{op}}^p.

The coefficient representations are the finite matrix/free-factor models specified by the formal theorem.

Proof idea

Keep the chronological exploration positions in the path counts. The weighted count, combined with the repaired coefficient estimate, bounds the normalized path sum. Summing the mixed-length contributions yields the trace comparison.

The accompanying iterated-expectation theorem applies an admissible one-pair comparison across distinct tensor legs to control a norm expectation.

Relation to the main channel route

The sharper threshold is a separate proved endpoint. The prescribed-channel assembler actually references the 2^{80}p^{80} one-pair version, which is sufficient for its chosen size. These two routes are shown separately in the proof map.

See Haar moments and path counting for the defect and dimension conventions.

Formal scope and manuscript correspondence

The actual one-pair trace bound is proved when the true matrix dimension D=N+1 satisfies D >= 2^32 p^80. It supplies the premise of the iterated estimate for finite matrix coefficients and remaining free-group factors. The abstract arbitrary traced C*-algebra formulation is outside the formalized scope. The revised manuscript restricts its one-pair argument to finite-support matrix/free-factor polynomials in the iteration algebras.

The included manuscript is the revised source incorporating the two counting repairs. Correspondence entries are reading aids rather than an exhaustive statement-equivalence certificate.
