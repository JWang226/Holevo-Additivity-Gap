Result catalog / guaranteed-gap-fraction

exact endpoint

Output-normalized gap lower limit and its large-K expansion

proved

Normalize the proved gap by output qubits and quantify how its guaranteed fraction depends on K.

Mathematical statement

The prescribed family satisfies

\liminf_n\frac{\Delta_\chi(T_{K,n})}{q_{\mathrm{out}}(T_{K,n})} \ge r_K=\frac1K-\frac{2\ln(1+9/K)}{\ln K}.

For real K>1, the scalar expansion has an explicit remainder:

0\le r_K-\frac{1-18/\ln K}{K} \le\frac{162}{K^2\ln K}.

Meaning of the fraction

If \delta_K>0, then r_K>0: the proved gap occupies a positive fraction of output-qubit cost at fixed K. The guarantee decreases with the large-K scale; the theorem does not claim a uniform positive fraction as K grows.

Proof idea

Divide the main gap lower bound by n\log_2K and let the finite-size correction vanish. The scalar identity cancels the base conversion factors. Elementary bounds on \ln(1+9/K) give the remainder estimate.

This is a lower guarantee, not an exact value of the channel's gap fraction. See the scaling chapter.

Formal scope and manuscript correspondence

The large-K remainder is bounded explicitly between zero and 162/(K^2 ln K). The numerical illustrative footnote is not separately certified.
