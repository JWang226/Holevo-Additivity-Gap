The correct exploration budget is defect plus two; a formal counterexample explains why the extra event is necessary.

The replacement estimate

For a nonempty exploration path, write \delta for the integer defectTwice, twice its graph defect. Then

\#\{\text{important exploration times}\}\le\delta+2.

The extra endpoint event must remain in the encoding budget.

Why the change is necessary

The library contains a literal path violating the older intermediate count, together with a path attaining the repaired bound. This rules out repairing the argument merely by restating the smaller budget.

The revised manuscript incorporates the replacement. The correction concerns an intermediate proof estimate, while the final prescribed channel dimensions remain as displayed in the main theorem.

Downstream use

Encoding important times gives defect-class counts. Retaining chronological positions also gives the weighted count used by the sharp Haar route. Those counts feed the moment comparison.

Read Haar moments for the role of path defect and the second correction for coefficient accounting.

Formal scope and manuscript correspondence

The closed reduced generator-balanced six-step word abaBAA has integer defect delta=length+singletons-2*vertices=2 and four important times. The bound delta+1 fails even excluding the final traversal; delta+2 is proved. The weighted chronological encoding preserves the final moment threshold. This repairs an intermediate counting claim used in the manuscript proof, not the main channel statement. The revised manuscript incorporates this repair.
