The broader question

One can ask for the original undamped realization in every sufficiently large local dimension, rather than a suitable finite realization at a specified or chosen size.

The phrases “there exists a finite realization,” “there is a prescribed sufficient size,” and “every sufficiently large size works” have different quantifiers.

What is established here

The prescribed theorem gives its displayed explicit dimensions. The qualitative theorem gives a finite realization using a separate deterministic damping construction.

Neither endpoint should be read as a proof of the stronger undamped all-dimensions assertion. This correspondence entry has no attached formal theorem proving it.

See reading Lean hypotheses and the formal scope notes below for the boundary.

Formal scope and manuscript correspondence

The stated generality for every kappa > 1 and all sufficiently large integer dimensions has not been formalized. The prescribed-dimension and alternative qualitative endpoints are proved and do not assume this broader claim.
