Free-group Collins-Youn bound

proved

The free-group comparison bounds an operator norm by a Hilbert–Schmidt coefficient norm, setting the scale for the channel certificate.

Mathematical statement

For K\ge2, n\ge1, and a traceless coefficient matrix, the normalized free tensor polynomial has operator norm at most c(K,n) times the Hilbert–Schmidt norm, where

c(K,n)^2=\frac{(1+9/K)^n-1}{K^n}.

The coefficient matrix is the observable appearing in the channel-adjoint problem.

Proof idea

Creation and annihilation estimates control the one-factor expression. Tensor induction retains the improved coefficient scale across the block factors. This establishes the comparison in the free regular representation.

Role in the finite construction

The free model supplies a tractable target norm. The finite Haar comparison approximates that target and produces a finite realization with adjoint coefficient \kappa_n c(K,n).

This is a proved operator estimate, not a hypothesis left in the main channel endpoint. The certificate and entropy consequence explain its downstream use.

Formal scope and manuscript correspondence

The normalized free-operator inequality is proved for all K >= 2 and n >= 1.
