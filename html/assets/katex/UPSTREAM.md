# KaTeX distribution

Unmodified JavaScript, stylesheet, and fonts from the official KaTeX 0.19.0 release:

https://github.com/KaTeX/KaTeX/releases/tag/v0.19.0

Archive: https://github.com/KaTeX/KaTeX/releases/download/v0.19.0/katex.tar.gz

Archive SHA-256: 966d9c85655081cca9a57e8a1d667f13f96b1c85c3f93655a8acef74c4ce3875

The original MIT license was retrieved unchanged from the tagged source and
is included in LICENSE. KaTeX is used only to display explanatory mathematical
notation; it is not involved in proof checking. All assets are served locally,
so the reader site also works offline.
