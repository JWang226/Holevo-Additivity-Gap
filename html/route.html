Why the construction works

The proof, step by step.

Make every single-use output highly mixed, find an entangled input with a less mixed two-use output, and turn this entropy difference into a communication advantage.

Read the construction chapter for the formulas and the interactive proof map for the implemented Lean paths.

1Actual channels and entropy

Start with positive, trace-one complex matrices and Kraus maps satisfying the completeness equation. This makes the later witness a physical channel, with real output states and tensor products.

Entropy is computed from the eigenvalues, including zero eigenvalues. Finite ensembles define the Holevo quantity over the actual output range. The primitive entropy calculations use nats; the final information endpoints use bits.

The first useful inequality is S_{\mathrm{nats}}(\sigma)\ge-\ln\operatorname{Tr}\sigma^2. It gives a route from a tractable quadratic output estimate to an entropy estimate.

Read finite channels, Holevo information, and purity and output entropy. Formal objects include DensityMatrix, KrausChannel, and Ensemble.information.

2Free comparison and finite Haar estimates

The channel's adjoint is represented by a polynomial in its unitary family. In the free regular model, the Collins–Youn bound controls that polynomial by a Hilbert–Schmidt coefficient norm with scale c(K,n).

To make one finite sample work for all observables, reduce the sphere of normalized traceless Hermitian matrices to a finite net. Encode the net tests into a common polynomial, use two-generator word reductions, and linearize. These reductions keep coefficient sizes and approximation losses explicit.

Finite Haar moment estimates then compare the reduced polynomial with its free model. They supply a quantitative expectation bound rather than leaving a generic norm-convergence assertion as a premise.

Read free operators and finite Haar matrices, the net certificate, and universal factorization. The net reduction is exists_net_linearization.

3Repaired combinatorial counts

The Haar trace expansion groups word contributions into exploration paths. Counting those paths and bounding their operator coefficients are separate tasks.

The correct important-event budget is \delta+2, for integer defect \delta. Marked-position accounting and first/last-occurrence contractions give the repaired coefficient exponent 9\delta+11. Literal formal counterexamples explain why the old shortcuts cannot be retained.

These replacements are incorporated in the revised manuscript. Chronological weighted counting also proves the separate sharp one-pair endpoint at D\ge2^{32}p^{80}. The main prescribed-channel route uses the sufficient 2^{80}p^{80} version.

Read Haar moments. Exact landmarks are card_importantTimes_le, coefficient_bound, and the sharp one-pair theorem.

4Prescribed dimensions and the channel witness

Choose the local size N=\lceil\exp(40(7\ln K+2)n)\rceil and the proof's moment order. The threshold n\ge n_0(K) makes all coefficient-size, moment, and tolerance requirements compatible.

explicitHaarExpectation supplies the finite norm comparison. A favorable sample for the combined net polynomial then yields the uniform adjoint estimate with t=\kappa_n c(K,n).

The certificate theorem accepts an expectation comparison as a premise; the final channel assembler supplies that premise. This explains why the map can show expectation and certificate results as parallel inputs to the assembler.

The main endpoint retains one actual channel's input dimension 2N^nK^{2n}, output dimension K^n, and a positive single-use lower bound. Read the construction chapter and favorable-realization probability.

5Conversion and qualitative separation

The certificate makes every single-use output highly mixed. The Bell witness supplies one less mixed joint output for the complementary channel paired with its conjugate.

The switch puts those two channels into branches of one map. The Weyl extension supplies orbit ensembles with maximally mixed average, converting the entropy contrast into a single-use Holevo upper bound and a two-use lower bound.

block_converted_bounds_of_certificate performs this conversion. The all-use Weyl identity proves the equality at every positive power, allowing entangled inputs.

A separate qualitative route uses finite-moment damping and slightly perturbed branch weights. A strict Bell entropy reserve absorbs the damping loss, giving the exact qualitative lower bound at every allowed block length. Read switch/Weyl extensions and qualitative realization.

6Operational coding and capacity

The entropy inequalities become communication statements through a separately defined capacity. Physical encoders prepare actual density matrices; POVM decoders have positive effects summing to the identity; their error is a Born probability.

HSW achievability constructs codes from finite ensembles. Blocking and padding realize positive-power rates at all sufficiently large physical lengths. A finite-code weak converse bounds the message count with an error correction that vanishes at the rate level.

The resulting coding equality gives C(T)\ge\chi(T\otimes T)/2, so the capacity gain is at least half the two-use gap.

Read operational capacity and the capacity chapter. This advantage over single-use Holevo information does not assert nonadditivity of already regularized capacity for distinct channels.

7Asymptotics and precise scope

At fixed K with positive \delta_K, increasing the construction parameter gives an output-linear gap and a gap of order the square root of input-qubit cost. The exact dimension calculation includes the local-size ceiling.

With growing K and n_K=\lceil K/\sqrt{\ln K}\rceil, a different limit makes single-use information vanish and two-use information and capacity diverge. Its input cost satisfies q_{\mathrm{in}}/K^2\to280/\ln2.

For one fixed channel, merely regrouping its uses has normalized two-block gap tending to zero. The growing families therefore require changing channels and dimensions.

Read the scaling chapter, dimension costs, and fixed-channel regrouping. Broad generic convergence predicates and other background generalizations remain outside the completed formal endpoints, as recorded in their scope entries.
