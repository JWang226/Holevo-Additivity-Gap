v1.0.0

Lean 4 certificates for the principal results in *Unbounded Holevo additivity gaps in finite dimensions*, with an offline proof browser, manuscript mappings, reproduction commands, and retained verification evidence.

The six challenge roots cover prescribed channel dimensions, the operational coding identity, small one-use Holevo information with large capacity gain and two-use ratio, Weyl extension identities for positive tensor powers, and two input-cost bounds. The full manuscript is not formalized. See formalization scope and statement correspondence review for qualifications.

The revised manuscript incorporates the two counting repairs recorded during formalization.
