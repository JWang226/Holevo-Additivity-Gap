proved

Prescribed finite dimensions and Holevo gap

Actual finite Kraus CPTP channel; the only non-typeclass hypotheses are K >= 2 and n >= n0(K). The theorem also proves positivity and the supplementary single-use lower bound 2n/K on the same witness. The intermediate combinatorial proofs are corrected without changing the final dimension formula.

proved

Operational classical capacity equals regularized Holevo information

Capacity is independently defined by actual density-matrix codewords, normalized POVMs, and vanishing average Born error at all positive block lengths. The coding theorem is proved, not assumed or used as the definition of capacity.

proved

Small positive single-use information with large capacity gain and two-use ratio

Combines and strengthens the two existence conclusions. The qualitative route uses deterministic finite moments, branch weights, and damping.

proved

Weyl-extension Holevo equality at every positive tensor power

Extends the one-use displayed equality to all positive tensor powers and operational capacity. Lean index n denotes n+1 channel uses. Arbitrary entangled inputs are included; the entropy endpoint is an infimum, without assuming minimum attainment.

proved

Input-qubit cost of the growing-K separation

For the actual prescribed family with nK=ceil(K/sqrt(ln K)), q_in/K^2 tends to 280/ln 2. The theorem supplies two-sided chi bounds and lower bounds for half chiTwo and operational capacity in terms of sqrt(log2 q_in). The threshold holds eventually for this same rounded nK.

corrected

Corrected important-exploration-time count

The closed reduced generator-balanced six-step word abaBAA has integer defect delta=length+singletons-2*vertices=2 and four important times. The bound delta+1 fails even excluding the final traversal; delta+2 is proved. The weighted chronological encoding preserves the final moment threshold. This repairs an intermediate counting claim used in the manuscript proof, not the main channel statement. The source manuscript is preserved and still needs editorial correction.

The main formal statement

This is the literal declaration header from HaarPrescribedBound.lean. Its ambient namespace, imports, and local settings remain visible on the full source page.

theorem exists_prescribed_channel_with_lower_bound {K n : ℕ}
    (hK : 2≤K) (hn : Quantitative.n₀ K≤n) :
    ∃ T : ActualConsequences.FiniteQuantumChannel,
      Fintype.card T.Input = 2*(localDimension K n)^n*K^(2*n) ∧
      Fintype.card T.Output = K^n ∧
      0 < T.chi ∧
      2*(n:ℝ)/(K:ℝ) ≤ T.chi ∧
      T.chi ≤ (n:ℝ)*Scalar.aK K+2*Scalar.log2 (kappa n) ∧
      (n:ℝ)*Scalar.log2 K/(K:ℝ)≤T.chiTwo ∧
      (n:ℝ)*Scalar.deltaK K-4*Scalar.log2 (kappa n)≤T.gap
