# Coding and the operational gain

The entropy inequalities become communication statements through a separately defined capacity. Physical encoders prepare actual density matrices; POVM decoders have positive effects summing to the identity; their error is a Born probability.

HSW achievability constructs codes from finite ensembles. Blocking and padding realize positive-power rates at all sufficiently large physical lengths. A finite-code weak converse bounds the message count with an error correction that vanishes at the rate level.

The resulting [coding equality](lean:Nonadditivity.Operational.operationalCapacity_eq_regularizedHolevoSupremum) gives $C(T)\ge\chi(T\otimes T)/2$, so the capacity gain is at least half the two-use gap.

Read [operational capacity](concept:operational-capacity) and [the capacity chapter](guide:capacity). This advantage over single-use Holevo information does not assert nonadditivity of already regularized capacity for distinct channels.
