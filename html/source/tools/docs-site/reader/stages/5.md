# Convert entropy to information

The certificate makes every single-use output highly mixed. The Bell witness supplies one less mixed joint output for the complementary channel paired with its conjugate.

The switch puts those two channels into branches of one map. The Weyl extension supplies orbit ensembles with maximally mixed average, converting the entropy contrast into a single-use Holevo upper bound and a two-use lower bound.

[block_converted_bounds_of_certificate](lean:Nonadditivity.BlockConstruction.block_converted_bounds_of_certificate) performs this conversion. The [all-use Weyl identity](result:weyl-all-uses) proves the equality at every positive power, allowing entangled inputs.

A separate qualitative route uses finite-moment damping and slightly perturbed branch weights. A strict Bell entropy reserve absorbs the damping loss, giving the exact qualitative lower bound at every allowed block length. Read [switch/Weyl extensions](concept:weyl-switch) and [qualitative realization](result:qualitative-realization).
